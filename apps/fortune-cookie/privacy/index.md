*Last updated 4 November 2026*

Fortune Cookie collects no data. It goes online only when you import quotes from Wikiquote.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.** The app doesn't talk to any server of mine.
- **Importing from Wikiquote.** When you use File > Import Fortunes…, the app sends what you type, and the page you choose, to Wikiquote, which the Wikimedia Foundation runs under its [privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). It sends no cookies and nothing about you.
- **Your things stay on your Mac.** Your saved fortunes, the fortunes you've read, your own batches, and your settings are stored on your Mac, where the app, its widget, and its Shortcuts actions can read them. They stay there even if you delete the app, in the folder `~/Library/Group Containers/2676VD986F.com.christiancable.fortune`.
- **Sharing is up to you.** When you share a fortune, it goes only where you choose to send it, through macOS's own Share menu.
- **Siri and Spotlight.** If you use them with Fortune Cookie, they work through Apple's own services, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If this ever changes, I'll update this page and the app's App Store listing first.
