Fortune Cookie is a little Mac app that brings back `fortune`, the Unix program that greeted people at login with a quip, a quote, or a groan. Open it and a cookie opens itself to show your fortune and its lucky numbers.

## Help

Something not working, or a fortune you'd rather not see? Email me at [christiancable@gmail.com](mailto:christiancable@gmail.com?subject=Fortune%20Cookie) and I'll get back to you. Fortune Cookie is made by one person, so it may take a day or two.

### Where are my saved fortunes?

In the Cookie Jar: click the box in the toolbar. Your saved fortunes, your own batches, and your settings stay on your Mac.

### How do I stop the fortune at login?

Turn off **Settings > General > Show a fortune when I log in**, or remove Fortune Cookie in System Settings > General > Login Items.

### How do I choose which fortunes I get?

**Settings > Batches** turns batches on and off and can limit you to short fortunes.

## Privacy

Fortune Cookie collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
